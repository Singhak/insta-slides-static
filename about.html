About Insta Slides

Insta Slides is a lightweight, browser-based tool for turning ideas into social media carousel slides. Edit slide text, choose a visual style, preview the deck, and download image files with a caption.

Designed to run in your browser

Editing, project import and export, pre-export checks, and image generation happen locally in your browser. AI generation is optional and connects directly to the provider or local model you choose.
